# Daylight

Daylight is a calendar planning assistant that checks Google Calendar and Gmail to help organize your schedule.

## What Daylight Can Do

- Look up events on your Google Calendar.
- Search Gmail for event details and commitments.
- Find conflicts and suggest free time.
- Add or delete calendar events after you confirm.

## Test Prompts

1. Is there anything on my calendar tomorrow?
2. Check my email and calendar, then tell me when I have two free hours for a drive this week.
3. Find a free two-hour slot for a workout this Sunday and ask me before adding it to my calendar.
